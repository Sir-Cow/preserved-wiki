# Team Preserved

---

Welcome to the official site for Team Preserved!

Starting in 2024, it began with **Preserved: Inferno**. A Minecraft mod with a few small changes we wanted to make to the game's progression. Fast forward to the present day, we have grown the core mod substantially and expanded our collection of projects which we are passionate about bringing to life for everyone to enjoy.

!!! warning
    Documentation is currently work in progress. Information may be missing or incomplete.
