# Team Preserved

---

Welcome to the official site for Team Preserved! Here you can find a collection of our projects. Documentation is currently work in progress. Information may be missing or incomplete.

## [Inferno](inferno/overview.md)

---

Enhances the "survival" aspect of Minecraft's gameplay. Core mod for the Preserved mod set.

## Torrential

---

Adds new mechanics to Minecraft's fishing system to give it more depth and rewards.

## Sunshine Grace

---

Adds a "grace period" for players who are joining an existing world for the first time.

## Reverb Compass

---

Adds waypoint teleportation to the game as a vanilla-esque feature.

## Bundle Auto Funnel

---

Automatically sorts items you pick up into your bundles.
